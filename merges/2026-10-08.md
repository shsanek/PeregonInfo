## 41. Rivers: transparent water, visible bed, riffle foam, weather

15:11 · WATER

Коммит: [a3c1aaf6c1](https://github.com/shsanek/EXEcutor/commit/a3c1aaf6c1)

MAP eye-level review: the river geometry was natural but the water was opaque and one flat colour. Water is now a separate transparent pass with its own shader (ps_2_0 59/64): a 2 m water mesh with vertex-baked depth (water level minus the drawn bed), so shallow water shows the bed with a slight green-brown tint and deep pools go dark; fresnel sky reflection from the LIGHT2 sky colours; sun and moon glints through the baked tree/terrain shadows; ripples and foam streaks scrolled along the map flow field.
