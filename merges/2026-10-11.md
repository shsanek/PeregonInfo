## 134. Rivers carved along the flow: water never above the banks, never interrupted, checked by a river validator

01:34 · RV1 RIVERS

Коммит: [239bb6cb83](https://github.com/shsanek/EXEcutor/commit/239bb6cb83)

Owner: 'in places the river sticks out of its bed; the bed need not go down but must never rise up; the river must never be interrupted'. Root causes found by the new validator (tools/map/rivercheck.py, R1+R2 before: 3198 violations): the level was set from two bank samples per 8 m station and a running minimum, so the water stood over lower floodplain and fell in 1.3 m stair steps (white 'waterfalls' on the Bystraya); the soft bank dipped 15 cm under the water at the edge; tributaries were raised to their parent's level; the carve was capped at depth+1 m, so beds stayed above the water where the line crossed higher ground (1031 dry samples); creeks narrower than the 2 m vertex grid got no channel; tile seams averaged away one-vertex creeks; nearest-segment frames left creases and 6 m cliffs in tight meanders; at 4 m and 8 m terrain LOD narrow channels vanished; nothing protected the water after roads and pads. The new flow pass in tools/map/rivers.py: the level comes from the natural ground on the real bank line and over the whole water-mesh footprint; it is never above it, never rises downstream and falls at most at the class slope_max (steps at riffles). A tributary lower than its parent caps the parent, a higher one is graded down into it. The bed is carved against the rendered water level with no cap and a 1.5 m minimum half-width. Banks are a bankfull cut, then a gentle valley slope, then one smoothing pass; every reach of every river is carved. A final guard keeps every vertex under the water mesh outside the channels above the water. The water grid is computed with an apron so tile seams agree, and its nodes are capped to the ground or road under them. Patch edges stay at 2 m across tiles, and LOD channel nodes keep water visible at a distance. No hummocks under water. After: 0 violations on R1+R2 and on the synthetic crossing map (1503 before). Every mapgen build writes rivers_baked.json; the LK1 lake can reuse caps/level_profile to route a river through the lake (docs/rivers.md).

## 136. Tree shadows over building shadows: no more lighter patches or dark outlines; building shadows stay dark to their tip

01:54 · SH3 SHADOWS

Коммит: [ef9b08c8d7](https://github.com/shsanek/EXEcutor/commit/ef9b08c8d7)

Cause: every caster wrote one shadow depth map per cascade, and the LS2 airy term let up to 25 % of the sun through whenever the nearest blocker was 3-12 m above the ground. The map did not know whether that blocker was a crown or a roof. A tree crown in front of a building hid the building and leaked light into its shadow (lighter patches, bare winter branches as light streaks); tall roofs leaked into the far end of their own shadows, so lower crowns read darker there; and the blocker average mixed roof and crown depths along crown edges, drawing a band along the tree outline. Clouds, the baked cache, sky occlusion, GI and the cab map were checked and are not involved. Fix: each cascade map now holds two layers in one atlas, solid casters (buildings, terrain, truck, people, trunks) and leaf cards at half resolution, filled in the same pass. The solid layer is filtered as before with no leak; the airy leaf layer multiplies it only where the solid layer still lets light through. A crown over a building shadow adds nothing, tall shadows are dark to the tip, crowns alone stay airy. Grass and leaf receivers use a half-tap variant to keep the cost down. Win32 SM2 bytes unchanged.
