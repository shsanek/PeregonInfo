## 240. The lowered plow digs the truck in: front axle loaded, nose down, the truck bogs against its own snow wall

01:54 · PLOWSINK

Коммит: [540b5708ff](https://github.com/shsanek/EXEcutor/commit/540b5708ff)

Ветка: `claude/peregon-plowsink`

Owner remark: the lowered blade only packed the snow. Plow resistance now comes from sourced terms — passive snow pressure on the cut face with Wong's snow strength parameters, snow momentum and the sliding windrow; the moldboard pushes the blade down into the frame, so the front axle loads (share 0.59 → 0.79), the nose pitches 2–4° down, the wheels spin and the truck stalls. The windrow is real snow laid in front of the blade, grows to a full moldboard and spills to the berms; when the truck backs off it stays behind as a wall. Numbers (1.6 t truck, 20 s full throttle, packed drift, blade down): 0.4 m — 72.6 → 12.5 km/h; 0.7 m and deeper — bogged; fresh snow bogs at ~0.9–1.0 m. The way to clear deep drifts is now skim first, then blade down, backing off and charging again. The quest drifts were lowered to 0.95–1.05 m crests so the plow quest stays passable. Sources: Wong (Theory of Ground Vehicles), McKyes (Soil Cutting and Tillage), Cuffey &amp; Paterson, CRREL monographs and reports, Kempainen et al. 1998.

## 241. Bush and raspberry simplification measured — no gain worth keeping, reverted

02:53 · BUSHPERF

Коммит: [c59e4932d7](https://github.com/shsanek/EXEcutor/commit/c59e4932d7)

Ветка: `claude/peregon-bushperf`

Owner: simplify bushes and raspberries, measure, keep only with a measurable gain. Baseline in the ripe raspberry thicket at 1080p: 240k prop triangles, 2 050 draws, GPU 16.0 ms (GPU-bound). A version that keeps the look (3-row leaflets, coarser berries; thicket 240k → 160k triangles) gained only 0.13 ms GPU on average — under the 0.2 ms threshold — so it was reverted. A harsher version gained ~0.3 ms but visibly changed the bushes, rejected. Finding: triangles are not the lever; the thicket's cost is draw count and sun-shadow passes — the raspberry LOD task is re-scoped that way. Card shrubs are already 160–310 triangles.

- ✓ Measurement: interleaved base/simple pairs, props-hidden control within 0.06 ms, sim hashes identical
- ✗ Result: not kept: 0.13 ms (0.8 %) is below the 0.2 ms threshold
