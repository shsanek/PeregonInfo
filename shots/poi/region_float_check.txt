Each probe is a point on the bottom edge of a grounded box or cylinder, projected into the tour view.
sky: the pixel 3 px below it is sky or fogged far terrain colour; under: the camera ray through that pixel hits the drawn
terrain more than 0.3 m behind the object (we see ground behind it, i.e. a gap under it); above: the pixel 2 px above the
bottom edge (which must be the object) is sky (the object is drawn somewhere else, e.g. lifted); silhouette corners of
far low objects can hit the background, so up to 0.4 % of the probes may.
